PulseLink Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 5, 2026

1. Data We Collect

  • Account info (name, email/phone) when you sign in.
  • App telemetry (crash/analytics) to improve stability.
  • Optional sync data (messages, contacts, themes) when you enable sync/remote web access.
  • Device data needed for core features (e.g., network state, app version).

2. How We Use Data

  • Provide messaging, emergency, and safety features.
  • Sync your settings/messages across signed-in devices when you opt in.
  • Improve performance, debug issues, and prevent abuse.
  • Serve ads in free tiers where applicable.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your data. Limited sharing occurs with service providers (hosting, analytics, crash reporting, payments) under data-protection terms. We may disclose data to comply with law or protect users and the Service.

4. Security

We use industry-standard security controls. No system is 100% secure; keep devices and accounts protected.

5. Your Choices

  • Opt out of sync/remote access by disabling those settings in-app.
  • Manage notifications and data permissions at the OS level.
  • Request deletion by contacting support@pulselink.app.

6. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 16 (or as defined by local law).

7. Changes

We may update this Policy periodically. Continued use after changes signifies acceptance.

8. Contact

Questions? Email support@pulselink.app.
