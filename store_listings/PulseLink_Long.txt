PulseLink is your silent guardian. Using discreet voice triggers, it instantly escalates emergency alerts to your trusted contacts—even if your phone is locked or out of reach.

**KEY FEATURES:**

🗣️ **Voice Activated Safety**
Set a custom "Safe Phrase" (e.g., "Tell my sister to call me") and a "Danger Phrase" (e.g., "Red Alert"). PulseLink listens for these specific keywords to trigger the appropriate response.

🆘 **Emergency Alerts**
Instantly send SMS and data notifications to your designated Emergency Contacts. Alerts include your live location and a loud siren to attract attention (unless Silent Mode is active).

📍 **Live Location Sharing**
In an emergency, every second counts. PulseLink shares your real-time location with your safety network so they can find you or direct help to you.

🔊 **Do Not Disturb Override**
Critical alerts are designed to break through silent modes, ensuring your loved ones hear the alarm when it matters most.

✅ **Safety Check-Ins**
Send quick "I'm safe" updates to your Check-In circle with a simple voice command, perfect for arriving home late or finishing a solo trip.

🌐 **Reliable Messaging**
Uses a smart hybrid system that tries internet messaging first (for speed) and falls back to SMS (for reliability) to ensure your alert gets through.
