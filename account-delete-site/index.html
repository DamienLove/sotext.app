Delete your account

Deleting your account removes your profile, linked contacts, synced messages (if enabled), and device data from our servers. Some operational logs and backups may be retained for up to 30 days.

If you cannot sign in, email us at pulselink-support@dmnlat.com with subject PulseLink Account Deletion Request.
