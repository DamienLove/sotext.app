## The Story

**The Problem**
In an emergency, every second counts. But traditional safety tools fail in two ways:
1.  **They are inaccessible**: You have to unlock your phone and dial numbers while panicked.
2.  **They are ignored**: Calls go to voicemail because people keep their phones on "Do Not Disturb" (DND).

**The Solution: PulseLink**
We have built a revolutionary Android app that solves both.
*   **Passive Voice Activation**: Just say your safe phrase, and help is on the way.
*   **True DND Override**: We use advanced Android audio permissions to break through silent modes, ensuring your trusted contacts actually *hear* you.

**Where We Are**
We have a working Beta! The core technology—including the "Firebase-First" delivery system and the "Channel Bypass" for Android 15—is built and tested. We also have "Beacon," a companion SMS app that integrates safety directly into your daily chats.
