# Video Ad Scripts

## Video 1: "The Meeting" (15s)
**Scene**: A busy boardroom. A phone is face down on the table.
**Audio (SFX)**: Muffled talking.
**Visual**: Text overlay: "Phone on Silent. Important Meeting."
**Action**: Suddenly, the phone BLARES a distinct, loud siren loop. Everyone looks.
**Visual**: Close up of phone screen: "EMERGENCY - DAD".
**Voiceover**: "Real emergencies don't care about meetings. PulseLink overrides Do Not Disturb so you never miss the call that matters."
**End Card**: PulseLink Logo. "Download on Google Play."

## Video 2: "The Walk Home" (30s)
**Scene**: POV shot. Walking down a dimly lit street. The breathing is a bit fast.
**Voiceover**: "Walking home alone shouldn't feel like a mission."
**Action**: The user whispers "Red Alert" (or specific phrase).
**Visual**: The phone in hand silently vibrates. Screen shifts to "SOS Active".
**Cut To**: A roommate on the couch. Their phone alarms loudly. They see the map location.
**Voiceover**: "With PulseLink's passive voice detection, help is just a phrase away. Even if you can't touch your screen."
**Visual**: Roommate texts "I see you. On my way."
**End Card**: PulseLink. Your Safety Net.
